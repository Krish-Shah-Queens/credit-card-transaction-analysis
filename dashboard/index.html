KNOW YOUR DATA

The context behind the numbers.

Real European credit card transactions from the ULB / Worldline dataset, observed over approximately two days in September 2013.

What the filters mean

Hours count forward from the first transaction. They are not local times of day; the final hour is partial. Minimum and maximum amounts are inclusive. Fraud comparisons show both labels within the selected time and amount range, even when a single label is selected elsewhere.

What you can conclude

Amounts are transaction values, not measured revenue or financial losses. The CSV has no currency field, customer identifiers, merchant categories, or calendar timestamps. V1–V28 are anonymized PCA components. These observations cannot establish seasonal trends or explain causes of fraud.

Quality & calculations

Exact duplicate rows and zero amounts are retained. The interactive data groups identical amounts by elapsed hour and label; counts, sums, medians and percentiles are computed exactly from their frequencies. A “typical” transaction means the median. Undefined rates display a dash.
